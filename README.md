## Project Process

1. **Contact** — Phone, WhatsApp (+91 98300 59421), or inquiry form
2. **Free 45-minute Consultation** — At Tollygunge studio or at your site
3. **Concept Brief** — Mood boards, space plan, and budget estimate
4. **Detailed Design** — Material selection, lighting design, custom furniture
5. **Execution** — Civil coordination, procurement, installation
