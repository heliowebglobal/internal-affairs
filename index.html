Our Process

How It Works

A simple 5-step process from your first idea to final handover.

01

Consultation

Get in touch via phone, WhatsApp, or our inquiry form. Share your project details — property type, location, size, and budget.

02

Concept

We schedule a free 45-minute consultation at our studio or your site. You receive mood boards, a space plan, and a budget estimate.

03

Detailed Design

Material selection, lighting design, and custom furniture. You approve every detail before execution begins.

04

Execution

Our in-house team handles civil coordination, procurement, and installation. We keep you updated at every milestone.

05

Handover

Final walkthrough and styling. Your space is move-in ready. We stay on for 30 days post-handover for touch-ups.

Typical timelines: 2BHK (8–12 weeks) · 3BHK (12–16 weeks) · Large projects (16–24 weeks)

Our Story

16 Years of Design Excellence

Founded in 2008, Internal Affairs began with a single principle: exceptional design should be accessible, transparent, and deeply personal.

What started in a modest studio in Tollygunge is now one of Kolkata's most respected design houses, with over 200 completed projects across residential, commercial, and architectural domains.

Our in-house team of designers, architects, and project managers ensures quality control at every stage — no outsourcing, no compromise.
