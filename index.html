Our Process

How It Works

A simple 5-step process from your first idea to final handover.

01

Consultation

Contact us via phone, WhatsApp, or inquiry form. Share your project details.

02

Concept

Free 45-minute consultation at our studio or your site. Mood boards & budget estimate.

03

Design

Material selection, lighting design, custom furniture. You approve every detail.

04

Execution

05

Handover

Final walkthrough and styling. Your space is move-in ready. 30 days post-handover support.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. Internal Affairs is a full-service interior design studio in Kolkata with over 16 years of experience across both residential and commercial projects. Our residential portfolio includes luxury apartments, penthouses, independent homes, and duplex villas across South Kolkata, New Town, Salt Lake, and Ballygunge. For commercial clients, we have designed corporate offices, co-working spaces, restaurants, retail boutiques, and institutional spaces.

Getting started is straightforward. Step 1 — Contact us via phone, WhatsApp (+91 98300 59421), or our inquiry form to share your project details (property type, location, approximate size, and budget range). Step 2 — We schedule a free 45-minute consultation at our Tollygunge studio or at your site. Step 3 — Our team develops an initial concept brief with mood boards, a space plan, and a budget estimate. Once you approve the concept, we move into detailed design, material selection, execution, and handover.

The timeline depends on the size and scope of work. For a 2BHK (approx. 900-1,100 sq ft) with a standard fit-out, complete execution — from design sign-off to handover — typically takes 8 to 12 weeks. A 3BHK or 4BHK with premium finishes and custom furniture usually requires 12 to 16 weeks. Large turnkey projects, penthouses, or commercial spaces can take 16-24 weeks.

Yes. Internal Affairs offers complete turnkey interior design services in Kolkata for both residential and commercial clients. A turnkey project means we manage the entire process end-to-end — from initial concept and space planning, through material sourcing, civil work coordination, furniture procurement, lighting design, and final styling — delivering a move-in-ready space. You have a single point of contact throughout the project.

Yes. We regularly incorporate Vastu Shastra principles into interior design for residential clients in Kolkata. Our designers are experienced in aligning spatial layouts, room orientations, colour schemes, and material choices with Vastu guidelines — without compromising on aesthetics or modern functionality. Vastu compliance is available as part of our full residential design service at no additional charge for the consultation phase.

Our studio works across the full spectrum of interior design styles and we do not impose a house aesthetic on our clients. We are equally fluent in contemporary and modern design (clean lines, open volumes, restrained material palettes), transitional interiors (a blend of classic warmth with modern simplicity), luxury and bespoke residential design, and industrial and biophilic commercial spaces.
